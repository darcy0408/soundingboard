*Last updated: July 13, 2026*

SoundingBoard is built to need as little of your data as possible. No account, no sign-up, no ads, no selling data.

## What the app processes

**Your conversation text.** When you practice or vent, the words you type or speak are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards them to two service providers to generate the experience: Anthropic (to generate the practice partner's and coach's responses, and your feedback report) and Cartesia (to turn responses into voice audio). We do not store your conversations on our servers, and we do not log their content. Our providers process this data to provide the service under their own data-processing terms.

**Responses you report.** If you flag an AI response as inappropriate, that response and the few messages just before it (the confirm dialog says exactly what's sent) are stored securely for up to 90 days so a human can review it. This is the one exception to "we don't store conversations," it only ever happens when you choose to report, and it's deleted automatically after review-window expiry.

**Your voice.** Speech is converted to text on your device using its built-in speech recognition (Apple's on iOS, Google's on Android). Audio recordings never leave your phone; only the resulting text is sent, as above.

**Your practice history.** Session setups, transcripts, and feedback reports are stored only on your device. Settings → "Delete all my data" erases them permanently. Deleting the app does the same.

**A random device identifier.** The app generates a random ID (not tied to your name, email, or store account) used to enforce fair-use rate limits on our server and attached to any response you report so we can spot abuse of the reporting feature.

**Purchases.** Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. We receive subscription status, never your payment details.

## What we don't do
- No accounts and no collection of your name, email, or contacts.
- No advertising, no trackers, no sale or sharing of personal data for marketing.
- No server-side storage of conversation content — except responses you explicitly report, kept up to 90 days for moderation review (see above).
- No use of your conversations to train AI models by us. (Provider handling is governed by their terms; we use API services that do not train on API data by default.)

## Children
SoundingBoard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data.

## A note on sensitive content
Practice conversations can touch on personal topics. Because processing happens transiently and history lives only on your device, the practical guidance is simple: don't include information you wouldn't say out loud — passwords, financial details, IDs. The app is a practice tool, not a records system.
