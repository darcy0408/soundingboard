## Description

Some conversations are too important to wing.

SoundingBoard lets you practice them out loud before they happen. Pick who you're talking to — a dismissive boss, a guilt-tripping parent, a defensive roommate — and rehearse against a realistic practice partner that actually pushes back. When you communicate well, it works. When you don't, you find out here instead of out there.

REHEARSE
• Choose a scenario: family, relationships, roommates, friendships, or work
• Set the other person's temperament and how hard they'll push
• Speak your side — push-to-talk, like a real conversation
• Effective moves land; vague complaints and heat don't. Just like real life.

GET COACHED
After every practice session you get a report: scores for clarity, composure, and assertiveness, the moments that worked, the moments to redo — with the exact words to try instead — and the one thing to practice next.

VENT FIRST, THEN FIND THE WORDS
Sometimes you need to let it out before you can say it well. Vent mode listens, helps you find what you're actually trying to say, then turns it into a practice session when you're ready.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. Your practice history stays on your device. Conversations are processed by an AI service to generate responses and aren't stored on our servers.

Three practice sessions free. SoundingBoard Pro unlocks unlimited sessions and full coaching reports: $9.99/month or $49.99/year.

SoundingBoard is a communication-practice tool. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, psychological, or emergency advice, and it does not diagnose, treat, or provide therapy for any condition.

## App Review notes (paste into App Store Connect review notes)

SoundingBoard is a communication-skills practice tool: users rehearse an upcoming difficult conversation (e.g., asking for a raise, setting a boundary with a parent) against an AI roleplay partner, then receive coaching feedback on clarity, composure, and assertiveness. It is not a therapy, mental-health, or companion app, and it makes no health claims.

AI content safety: all responses are generated server-side via Anthropic's Claude with a constrained system prompt (no slurs, no sexual content, PG-13 ceiling, personas are difficult but never abusive). User messages that indicate crisis are intercepted before reaching the model and receive a fixed message directing the user to crisis resources (988). Sessions have a hard turn cap. Conversations are not stored server-side.

Demo: complete onboarding (tap Agree & continue), choose "Rehearse a conversation" → Family → "Set a boundary with a parent" → Start. Type or hold the mic to speak. Tap "End & get feedback" to see the coaching report. No account or login exists.
